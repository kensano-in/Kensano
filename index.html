About Kensano

Kensano is a private digital archive dedicated to preserving unfinished letters, quiet poems, and stories that deserve to live beyond memory. The archive is invite-only during its early phase to ensure the integrity and authenticity of each manuscript.

Founders

Shinichiro — Vision & Archive Design
Ken — Systems & Preservation Technology

Together they are building a quiet home for stories that might otherwise be lost.

Manuscript Submission

If you have written a poem, diary, short story, or novel you wish to preserve inside Kensano, send your manuscript to:

admin@kensano.in

Include your name, short introduction, and manuscript file. Our review team will respond within 7–10 days.
